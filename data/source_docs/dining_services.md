# Berkeley Dining Services

_Last reviewed: 2026-07-30_

## Dining Halls
_Source: https://dining.berkeley.edu/_

UC Berkeley Dining operates several dining commons, including Crossroads, Café 3, Clark Kerr, and Foothill. Dining hall locations, meal plans, menus, and operating hours are published on the Berkeley Dining website.

## Vegetarian and Vegan Options
_Source: https://dining.berkeley.edu/_

Every campus dining commons offers vegetarian and vegan meal options. Daily menus can be viewed online and filtered by dietary preference, and in-hall signage marks vegetarian (V) and vegan (VG) items. Each dining commons also provides plant-based protein options, and allergen-friendly stations serve hot vegan meals daily.

## Water Refill Stations
_Source: https://dining.berkeley.edu/sustainability/_

Filtered water refill stations are located throughout campus in academic buildings, libraries, and other public facilities. The Berkeley Food Institute's Foodscape Map can be used to locate nearby refill stations and plan convenient places to refill reusable water bottles.
