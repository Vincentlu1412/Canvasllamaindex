# Berkeley Dining Services

## Dining Halls
Where are the major dining halls on campus? UC Berkeley Dining operates several dining commons, including Crossroads, Café 3, Clark Kerr, and Foothill. Information about dining hall locations, meal plans, menus, and operating hours is available on the Berkeley Dining website.

## Vegetarian and Vegan Options
Are there vegetarian or vegan dining options on campus? Yes, UC Berkeley Dining offers vegetarian and vegan meal options at all campus dining commons. Students can view daily menus online and filter meals by dietary preference, including vegetarian and vegan options. Menu signage also identifies vegetarian (V) and vegan (VG) items. In addition, each dining commons provides plant-based protein options, and allergen-friendly stations offer hot vegan meals daily.

## Water Refill Stations
Where can I refill my water bottle on campus? Filtered water refill stations are located throughout the UC Berkeley campus in academic buildings, libraries, and other public facilities. Students can use the Berkeley Food Institute's Foodscape Map to locate nearby water refill stations and plan convenient places to refill reusable water bottles while on campus.
