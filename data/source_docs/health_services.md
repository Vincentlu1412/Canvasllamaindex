# University Health Services (Tang Center)

_Last reviewed: 2026-07-30_

## Location
_Source: https://uhs.berkeley.edu/_

University Health Services (UHS), also known as the Tang Center, is located at 2222 Bancroft Way, Berkeley, CA 94720, on the southwest corner of the UC Berkeley campus between Ellsworth Street and Fulton Street (Oxford Street). It provides medical care, counseling, pharmacy, radiology, and other health services for students.

## Appointments & Care
_Source: https://uhs.berkeley.edu/_

Appointments with University Health Services are scheduled through the MyUCBerkeleyHealth online portal or by phone. Most medical services require an appointment, while urgent medical concerns can be addressed through Urgent Care or the 24/7 Advice Nurse without a scheduled visit.
