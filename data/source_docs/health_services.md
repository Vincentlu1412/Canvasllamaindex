# University Health Services (Tang Center)

## Location
University Health Services (UHS), also known as the Tang Center, is located at 2222 Bancroft Way, Berkeley, CA 94720. The Tang Center is situated on the southwest corner of the UC Berkeley campus between Ellsworth Street and Fulton Street (Oxford Street). It provides medical care, counseling, pharmacy, radiology, and other health services for students.

## Appointments & Care
How can I schedule an appointment with University Health Services? Students can schedule appointments through the MyUCBerkeleyHealth online portal or by calling University Health Services. Most medical services require an appointment, while urgent medical concerns can be addressed through Urgent Care or the 24/7 Advice Nurse.
