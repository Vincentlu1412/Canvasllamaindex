# UC Berkeley Library Services & Quiet Study Spaces

_Last reviewed: 2026-07-30_

## Library Overview
_Source: https://www.lib.berkeley.edu/_

The UC Berkeley Library system spans multiple library locations, collections, research support, and study spaces. Branch locations, service offerings, borrowing policies, and operating hours are listed on the official Library website.

## Moffitt Library Hours & Access
_Source: https://www.lib.berkeley.edu/_

Moffitt Library's hours vary throughout the academic year and during holidays, so students should check the Library Hours page for the current schedule before visiting. Student entry to Moffitt Library requires a physical Cal 1 Card scan.

## Borrowing Books
_Source: https://www.lib.berkeley.edu/_

Library materials are borrowed by presenting a Cal 1 Card. Loan periods and borrowing privileges depend on the borrower's affiliation and the type of material. Materials can be searched and requested through the UC Library Search system.

## Quiet Study Spaces
_Source: https://www.lib.berkeley.edu/_

Quiet study spaces are available throughout the UC Berkeley Library system, including Moffitt Library and other campus libraries, along with designated silent reading rooms and reservable study spaces listed on the Library website.
