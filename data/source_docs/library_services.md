# UC Berkeley Library Services & Quiet Study Spaces

## Library Information
Information about UC Berkeley libraries, including library locations, services, borrowing policies, collections, study spaces, research support, and operating hours, can be found on the official UC Berkeley Library website.

## Moffitt Library Hours & Access
Moffitt Library hours vary throughout the academic year and during holidays. Students should check the UC Berkeley Library Hours page for the most up-to-date schedule before visiting. Moffitt Library requires a physical Cal 1 Card scan for student entry.

## Borrowing Books
How do I borrow books from the UC Berkeley Library? Students can borrow library materials by presenting their Cal 1 Card. Loan periods and borrowing privileges vary depending on the user's affiliation and the type of material. Library materials can be searched and requested through the UC Library Search system.

## Quiet Study Spaces
Where can I find a quiet study space on campus? Quiet study spaces are available throughout UC Berkeley Libraries, including Moffitt Library and other campus libraries. Students can also use designated silent reading rooms and reservable study spaces listed on the UC Berkeley Library website.
