# UC Berkeley Recreational Sports (RSF)

## Recreational Facilities
What recreational facilities are available to students? UC Berkeley offers a variety of recreational facilities for students, including the Recreational Sports Facility (RSF), swimming pools, tennis courts, climbing walls, fitness centers, recreational fields, and outdoor adventure programs. Recreational Sports also offers group fitness classes, intramural sports, and sports clubs.

## RSF Access Policy
How can I access the Recreational Sports Facility (RSF)? Eligible UC Berkeley students can access the Recreational Sports Facility (RSF) using their Cal 1 Card. Membership is included for most registered students who pay the required campus fees. Visitors and affiliates may purchase memberships or day passes if eligible.
