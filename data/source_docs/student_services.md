# Student Support & Advising Services

## Academic Advising
Where can I find academic advising services? Academic advising is available through college advising offices, departmental advisors, and specialized student support programs. Students can locate the appropriate advising office based on their college or major through the UC Berkeley Academic Guide and advising websites.

## Cal Student Central
How do I contact Cal Student Central? Students can contact Cal Student Central by submitting an online case, scheduling a virtual or in-person appointment, or visiting the office during business hours. Cal Student Central assists with financial aid, billing, registration, residency, and enrollment services.

## Student Organizations & Events
Where can I find information about student organizations? Information about registered student organizations is available through the LEAD Center. Students can explore organizations by interest, attend involvement fairs, and learn how to join or start a student organization using the LEAD Center's online resources.
How can I find upcoming campus events? Upcoming campus events, lectures, workshops, performances, and student activities are listed on the official UC Berkeley Events Calendar. Students can search events by date, topic, audience, or campus department.
