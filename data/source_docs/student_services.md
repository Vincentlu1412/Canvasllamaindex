# Student Support & Advising Services

_Last reviewed: 2026-07-30_

## Academic Advising
_Source: https://academic-guide.berkeley.edu/_

Academic advising is available through college advising offices, departmental advisors, and specialized student support programs. Students can locate the appropriate advising office for their college or major through the UC Berkeley Academic Guide and individual advising websites.

## Cal Student Central
_Source: https://calstudentcentral.berkeley.edu/_

Cal Student Central can be contacted by submitting an online case, scheduling a virtual or in-person appointment, or visiting the office during business hours. It assists with financial aid, billing, registration, residency, and enrollment services.

## Student Organizations
_Source: https://lead.berkeley.edu/_

Information about registered student organizations is available through the LEAD Center. Students can explore organizations by interest, attend involvement fairs, and learn how to join or start a student organization using the LEAD Center's online resources.

## Campus Events
_Source: https://events.berkeley.edu/_

Upcoming campus events — lectures, workshops, performances, and student activities — are listed on the official UC Berkeley Events Calendar, searchable by date, topic, audience, or campus department.
