# Student Technology Services & Campus Wi-Fi

## Wi-Fi Connection (eduroam vs CalVisitor)
UC Berkeley provides several wireless networks, including eduroam, CalVisitor, and Berkeley-IoT. Students should connect to eduroam using their CalNet ID credentials for secure access to campus wireless services. 
Eduroam is a secure wireless network intended for UC Berkeley students, faculty, staff, and visitors from participating institutions using institutional credentials. Berkeley-Visitor (or CalVisitor) is designed for campus guests who do not have CalNet credentials and provides temporary internet access without requiring a university account.

## Printing and Scanning
Where can I print and scan documents on campus? Students can print and scan documents at multiple locations across campus, including UC Berkeley Libraries and designated student computing labs. Printing services are available through campus-managed printing systems, and some locations require a Cal 1 Card or CalNet authentication. Scanning services are available at many UC Berkeley library locations through self-service scanners and multifunction printers. Students can scan documents and save them to email, cloud storage, or a USB drive.

## Tech Support & CalNet Password Reset
Where can I get technical support for my laptop or software? Students can receive technical support through Student Technology Services (STS). STS provides assistance with personal devices, software installation, Wi-Fi connectivity, CalNet accounts, and other technology-related issues available to UC Berkeley students.
How do I reset my CalNet password? Students can manage their CalNet account, including resetting their password, through the CalNet Identity and Access Management website. Identity verification may be required before a new password can be created. The CalNet portal also allows users to manage two-step verification and other account security settings.

## Campus Computer Labs
Where can I find campus computer labs? Campus computer labs are available across UC Berkeley through libraries, academic departments, and Student Technology Services. Information about locations, available software, and operating hours can be found on the Student Technology Services website.
