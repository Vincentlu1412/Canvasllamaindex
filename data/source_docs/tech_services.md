# Student Technology Services & Campus Wi-Fi

_Last reviewed: 2026-07-30_

## Wi-Fi Networks (eduroam vs. CalVisitor)
_Source: https://technology.berkeley.edu/_

UC Berkeley provides several wireless networks, including eduroam, CalVisitor, and Berkeley-IoT. Eduroam is the secure wireless network for UC Berkeley students, faculty, staff, and visitors from participating institutions, and it is accessed by logging in with CalNet ID credentials. Berkeley-Visitor (CalVisitor) is designed for campus guests who do not have CalNet credentials and provides temporary internet access without requiring a university account.

## Printing and Scanning
_Source: https://technology.berkeley.edu/_

Printing and scanning are available at multiple locations across campus, including UC Berkeley Libraries and designated student computing labs. Printing runs through campus-managed printing systems, and some locations require a Cal 1 Card or CalNet authentication. Scanning is available at many library locations through self-service scanners and multifunction printers, with output saved to email, cloud storage, or a USB drive.

## Tech Support & CalNet Password Reset
_Source: https://sts.berkeley.edu/_

Student Technology Services (STS) provides technical support for personal devices, software installation, Wi-Fi connectivity, CalNet accounts, and other technology-related issues. CalNet password resets and account management, including two-step verification, are handled through the CalNet Identity and Access Management website; identity verification may be required first.

## Campus Computer Labs
_Source: https://sts.berkeley.edu/_

Campus computer labs are available across UC Berkeley through libraries, academic departments, and Student Technology Services. Lab locations, available software, and operating hours are listed on the Student Technology Services website.
