# Berkeley Parking & Transportation

## Parking
Where can I park on campus as a student or visitor? UC Berkeley offers parking for students, faculty, staff, and visitors in designated campus parking structures and lots. Visitors may use hourly parking where available, while students with eligible permits can park in assigned permit lots. Parking information, permit requirements, and visitor parking locations are provided by Berkeley Parking & Transportation.

## Shuttles & BART Access
Is there a campus shuttle service available? Yes, UC Berkeley operates campus shuttle services (Bear Transit) that connect various campus locations. Additional transportation services, including accessible transportation options, are available for eligible students. Shuttle routes and schedules are provided by Berkeley Parking & Transportation.
How can I travel from Downtown Berkeley BART station to the UC Berkeley campus? The Downtown Berkeley BART station is within walking distance of the UC Berkeley campus. Students may also use AC Transit buses or campus shuttle services to reach destinations across campus from the station.
