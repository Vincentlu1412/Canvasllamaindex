# Berkeley Parking & Transportation

_Last reviewed: 2026-07-30_

## Parking
_Source: https://pt.berkeley.edu/_

UC Berkeley offers parking for students, faculty, staff, and visitors in designated campus parking structures and lots. Visitors may use hourly parking where available, while students with eligible permits can park in assigned permit lots. Parking information, permit requirements, and visitor parking locations are provided by Berkeley Parking & Transportation.

## Shuttles & BART Access
_Source: https://pt.berkeley.edu/_

UC Berkeley operates campus shuttle services (Bear Transit) that connect various campus locations, including accessible transportation options for eligible students. Shuttle routes and schedules are provided by Berkeley Parking & Transportation. The Downtown Berkeley BART station is within walking distance of the UC Berkeley campus; AC Transit buses and campus shuttle services are also available to reach destinations across campus from the station.
